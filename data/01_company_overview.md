# Norvik Instruments — Company Overview

## About us
Norvik Instruments B.V. designs and sells the **Skylark** range of smart home weather stations. The company was founded in 2017 by Ilse Marchetti (CEO) and Tomasz Oduya (CTO) in Deventer, the Netherlands. Headquarters moved to Zwolle in March 2023 to make room for the in-house calibration lab.

As of Q3 2026, Norvik employs 142 people across three locations:

| Location | Function | Headcount |
|---|---|---|
| Zwolle (NL) | HQ, engineering, calibration lab | 96 |
| Gdańsk (PL) | Firmware and cloud platform team | 31 |
| Porto (PT) | Customer support (EN, NL, DE, PT, ES) | 15 |

## Products
- **Skylark Mini** — indoor/outdoor temperature and humidity. Launched 2019.
- **Skylark Pro** — adds barometric pressure, wind speed/direction, rain gauge and UV index. Launched 2021.
- **Skylark Pro+** — Pro plus a PM2.5/PM10 air-quality sensor and a solar panel option. Launched 2024.
- **Skylark Cloud** — subscription service for historical data, alerts and the public API.

## Key figures (FY2025)
- Revenue: EUR 38.4 million (FY2024: EUR 31.1 million)
- Units shipped: 212,000
- Active Skylark Cloud subscribers: 64,500
- Largest markets by revenue: Germany (31%), Netherlands (22%), UK (14%)

## Mission
Make hyper-local weather data accurate, affordable and open, so that households, schools and small farms can make better everyday decisions.
