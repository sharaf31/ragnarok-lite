# Warranty and Returns Policy

Effective: 1 February 2026. Replaces the policy dated 1 June 2023.

## 1. Standard warranty
All Skylark hardware sold in the EU and UK carries a **2-year limited warranty** from the date of delivery.
Products bought directly from norvik.example (the Norvik webshop) receive an **extended 3-year warranty** at no extra cost, provided the device is registered within 60 days of delivery.

## 2. What is covered
- Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship
- Sensor drift beyond twice the stated accuracy in the user manual
- Wind vane magnet failure (a known issue on hardware revision B, also covered on revision C)

## 3. What is not covered
- Damage from lightning, flooding, or mounting on unsuitable structures
- Battery leakage from alkaline batteries
- Cosmetic UV discolouration of the plastic housing after 3 years
- Devices opened or modified by anyone other than an authorised Norvik service partner

## 4. Returns (right of withdrawal)
- Webshop purchases may be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund, which is longer than the 14 days required by EU law.
- Items must be returned with all accessories. A restocking fee of **EUR 15** applies if the outdoor array has been mounted and shows signs of installation.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 10 business days of the return being received in Zwolle.

## 5. How to make a claim
1. Open a ticket at support.norvik.example with the device serial number (on the label inside the battery compartment).
2. Customer Support in Porto responds within **2 business days**.
3. If a replacement is approved, Norvik ships it first (advance replacement). Return the faulty unit within 21 days using the prepaid label.
4. If the faulty unit is not received within 21 days, the customer is invoiced the retail price of the replacement.

## 6. Changes from the 2023 policy
- The webshop return window increased from 14 to 30 days.
- Advance replacement is now standard. Previously it was only available to Skylark Cloud subscribers.
- The extended 3-year webshop warranty is new.
